Ep.16

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

In short: Ep.16 stores your email address and your diary so it can sync between your devices. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Ep.16 is a K-drama diary app for iPhone, made by Jimmy Nguyen, an independent developer (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What Ep.16 stores

Your account

  • Email address, to identify your account and to send you a code if you reset your password.
  • Password. It is stored only as a one-way hash by the sign-in service; nobody, including me, can read it.

Your diary

  • The dramas you add, with their status (watched, watching, want to watch), your ratings, notes, dates and episode progress.
  • Rewatches, with their dates, ratings and notes.
  • Your favorite actors.
  • A copy of each drama’s title, poster, genres, air date and episode count from TMDB, so your diary and Wrapped work quickly.

Your diary is private to your account. Each row is tied to your account, and the database only lets a signed-in account read or change its own rows.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign-in session on your device so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

What Ep.16 doesn’t collect

No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-tracking tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Services Ep.16 uses

  • Supabase hosts the sign-in system and the database that stores your account and diary.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) supplies drama, cast and image data. The app requests this data directly from TMDB, so TMDB receives your IP address and what you browse or search, as any website would. Nothing about your account or diary is sent to TMDB.
  • An email delivery service sends password reset codes to your email address.

These services handle data only to run Ep.16. I don’t sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

How long data is kept

Your account and diary are kept until you delete them.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and every diary entry, rewatch and favorite actor straight away. You can also email me to ask for deletion.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit everything you’ve logged in the app, and delete it at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Email me and I’ll help.

Children

Ep.16 isn’t directed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has made an account, email me and I’ll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to diaries is restricted per account in the database. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update it here and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
