I forgot my password

How do I delete my account?

Open Account → Delete account. Your account and your whole diary are deleted permanently, straight away.

A drama is missing or has wrong details

Drama, cast and image data come from The Movie Database (TMDB), which anyone can edit. Fixes made there show up in Ep.16.

Will my diary be on my other devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same email and your diary syncs.
